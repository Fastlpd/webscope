# EP-001 v02 — The House of Kings
**Status:** Creator review draft. This is the palace-first opening and supersedes v01 as the intended opening, but does not delete the legacy draft.  
**Format:** Illustrated cinematic drama; target 8–12 minutes depending on performance and edit.  
**Dramatic question:** Can a young heir love his family and still recognise that the kingdom they rule is failing people beyond its gates?  
**Emotional promise:** The audience should feel that the palace is a real home before it becomes the place Rex loses.  
**Core turn:** Rex sees the human cost of delayed flood-relief repairs and realises the palace's polished public story does not match what ordinary families are experiencing. Amara notices a documentary inconsistency.  
**Episode boundary:** Rex is not exiled in this episode. The exile is earned through a chain of choices across the season and reaches its decision in EP-007. Milo is a puppy here.

## Visual promise
Royal, majestic, intimate rather than gaudy: midnight navy, ivory limestone, antique gold used sparingly, carved dark wood, woven textiles, bronze details, soft morning light, candlelit evening interiors, elegant ceremonial dress. The city is alive and dignified, never reduced to misery. Use cinematic realism, natural faces, tactile materials, restrained camera movement and emotional close-ups.
