# EP-001 — The House of Kings
*Palace-first screenplay draft v02 | Creator review required*

## SCENE 1 — EXT. AVELOR PALACE — DAWN

A thin line of sunlight finds the palace roof before it reaches the city below. The palace is magnificent, but not cold: pale limestone, deep blue banners, carved doors, bronze lanterns and woven royal colours moving gently in the morning wind. Beyond the outer wall, Avelor City is already awake. A baker lifts a shutter. A bus exhales at a crowded stop. A woman carries two water containers past a wall bearing a faded notice about flood repairs.

The camera glides through the palace gates, across a courtyard where gardeners are at work, then into the family wing.

A palace bell rings once. The sound carries over the city.

## SCENE 2 — INT. PALACE CONSERVATORY — MORNING

REX, 22, sits on the stone floor in a tailored ivory shirt and dark trousers. A very young puppy, MILO, has fallen asleep against his shoe. Rex is trying to read a report while Milo worries the corner of the paper.

**REX** You're making a poor argument for literacy.

Milo tugs the page.

**REX** That is a royal document.

Milo tugs again.

**REX** And apparently you outrank the council.

PRINCESS AMARA, 19–20, enters with two cups. She watches the puppy win the argument.

**AMARA** He has better instincts than the council.

**REX** He also ate the tassel from my formal sash.

**AMARA** Then perhaps he is preparing you for public life.

She hands Rex a cup. Their ease is familiar, affectionate. Amara notices the report.

**AMARA** Is that the flood-repair update?

Rex nods. The warmth in his face settles.

**REX** It says the eastern district is complete.

**AMARA** It isn't.

**REX** You went there?

**AMARA** I went with the petitions clerk yesterday. Three streets still have temporary roofing. The clinic is using a room with a ceiling that leaks when it rains.

**REX** The report says the final payment was released.

**AMARA** The report says a lot of things.

She opens a folded note, not a dramatic secret document, just a list of addresses and dates.

**AMARA** These families are still waiting. I checked the public register. The completion date is the same on six separate invoices.

**REX** Could be a copied template.

**AMARA** It could. But the contractor's inspection was supposedly on a day the road was closed.

Rex looks again at the report. He does not leap to a conclusion.

**REX** We need to know whether the work was done before we accuse anyone of taking money.

**AMARA** I know. That's why I brought dates, not accusations.

Milo wakes, sneezes, and presses his nose into Rex's palm. Rex scratches his ear.

**REX** We'll check it properly.

## SCENE 3 — INT. PALACE FAMILY DINING ROOM — LATER

A long table, but breakfast is relaxed rather than ceremonial. QUEEN ELARA pours tea. KING EDRIC reads a briefing. PRINCE ADRIAN, 23–24, enters with the ease of someone who grew up in this house but still notices whose chair is nearest the king.

**ADRIAN** I see the youngest member of the household has already begun his campaign for influence.

Milo, now under Rex's chair, chews a soft toy.

**REX** He's building a coalition.

**AMARA** Against the furniture.

Adrian takes a piece of toast and sits beside Rex. For a moment, the family feels genuinely close.

King Edric closes the briefing.

**EDRIC** The relief programme will be discussed at council. The public expects the final phase to be completed before the Founder's celebration.

**REX** The eastern district isn't complete.

The king looks at him.

**EDRIC** The report says it is.

**AMARA** The families say otherwise.

Elara's hand pauses over the teapot. Adrian looks from Rex to the king.

**EDRIC** Reports are verified by officials. We cannot govern by collecting every complaint and treating it as fact.

**REX** We shouldn't govern by treating a signed report as more real than the people standing under a leaking roof.

A quiet beat. Rex has spoken respectfully, but the words land.

**EDRIC** You will learn that a kingdom cannot be run on instinct.

**REX** Then let me see the inspection records.

**EDRIC** The council will review them.

**AMARA** The register already has inconsistencies.

**EDRIC** Amara, you are not responsible for the treasury.

**AMARA** No. I'm responsible for noticing when people come to the palace and leave with the same problem they came with.

Elara intervenes gently.

**ELARA** We can ask for the records without turning breakfast into a hearing.

Adrian tries to lighten the air.

**ADRIAN** I vote we let Milo chair the meeting. He has already demonstrated an appetite for paperwork.

No one laughs immediately. Then Rex gives a small smile, grateful for the attempt. The tension does not disappear.

## SCENE 4 — EXT. AVELOR CITY — EASTERN DISTRICT — AFTERNOON

Rex and Amara arrive without a parade. They are accompanied at a respectful distance by CAPTAIN TOMAS, not a crowd of guards. The district is not a caricature of poverty. It is a working neighbourhood: a tailor repairs a school uniform, children play beside a shop, a woman sells fruit, a mechanic works beneath a raised vehicle. Some buildings show flood damage. Others have been repaired by their owners.

Rex listens more than he speaks.

A MOTHER, holding a toddler, shows them a room where a patch of ceiling has darkened after rain.

**MOTHER** They came once to take pictures. They said the work would begin the following week.

**REX** When was that?

**MOTHER** Six weeks ago.

**AMARA** Did anyone give you a reference number?

The mother finds a creased paper. Amara writes it down.

A LOCAL BUILDER approaches, wiping his hands.

**BUILDER** They paid for materials that never arrived. I was told not to ask questions if I wanted the next contract.

Rex does not promise a solution he cannot deliver.

**REX** I can't promise what the council will decide today. I can promise I will not tell them this work is finished.

The mother studies him.

**MOTHER** People come here and promise things. Then the cars leave.

Rex accepts the rebuke.

**REX** Then don't judge me by what I say here. Judge me by whether someone comes back.

On the way out, Rex sees a child helping an older neighbour carry a repaired water container. The moment is small, ordinary and dignified. He understands that the kingdom is not an abstraction. It is made of people who keep one another going.

## SCENE 5 — INT. PALACE ARCHIVE ANTECHAMBER — LATE AFTERNOON

AMARA meets MARA VENN, the palace archivist, in a room lined with carefully labelled ledgers. Amara asks to compare the public completion register with the original inspection schedule.

**MARA** I can show you what is public. The signed originals require a council request.

**AMARA** The public register lists six inspections on the same date.

Mara checks the page. Her expression changes by a fraction.

**MARA** That date was a closure day.

**AMARA** So it is an error?

**MARA** It is an inconsistency. Be careful with the difference.

Mara closes the ledger.

**MARA** If you are right, someone will want to know who noticed first.

Amara looks at her.

**AMARA** Then I should make sure the record is accurate before they ask.

Mara does not hand over a secret file. She writes down the formal process for requesting the originals.

## SCENE 6 — INT. KING'S STUDY — EVENING

King Edric stands at the window. LORD SOREN VALE, a polished adviser in his late forties, has laid a folder on the desk. Adrian is present, invited to observe the council briefing.

**SOREN** The heir's visit to the eastern district will be interpreted as a public challenge to the programme.

**EDRIC** He went to listen.

**SOREN** The public will not distinguish listening from accusation. The Founder's celebration is in ten days. We need a united account.

Adrian glances at the folder.

**ADRIAN** If the work is complete, the records should settle it.

Soren gives him a courteous smile.

**SOREN** Of course. And if a few clerical dates are untidy, we should not allow them to become a crisis.

Edric opens the folder. A draft statement says the programme has been completed and all complaints are being handled through normal channels.

**EDRIC** I will not have the family divided in public.

**SOREN** Then the family must speak with one voice.

Adrian sees the phrase “all inspections independently verified.” He hesitates, but says nothing. Edric notices.

**EDRIC** You have something to add?

**ADRIAN** No, Uncle. Not without seeing the records.

Soren closes the folder.

**SOREN** Sensible.

## SCENE 7 — INT. PALACE FAMILY CORRIDOR — NIGHT

Rex returns to the family wing. Milo, now clean and sleepy, toddles toward him with an awkward puppy run. Rex kneels to greet him.

Amara approaches with the copied dates.

**AMARA** Mara confirmed the date was a closure day.

**REX** Did she give you the originals?

**AMARA** No. She gave me the process. We need a formal request.

**REX** Good. We'll do it properly.

Amara looks down the corridor toward the king's study.

**AMARA** Do you think Father already knows?

Rex takes time before answering.

**REX** I think he knows the kingdom is under pressure. I don't know what else he knows.

**AMARA** And if he tells us to stop?

**REX** Then we'll have to decide whether being his children is more important than being honest.

Amara looks at him, unsettled by the cost hidden in that sentence.

Milo drops his toy at Rex's feet. Rex picks it up and gives it back.

**REX** Come on, little man. You have a kingdom to conquer.

Milo bounds after him, trips over his own paws, and gets up again.

## SCENE 8 — INT. KING'S STUDY — SAME NIGHT

After the corridor empties, Edric remains alone. He opens a locked drawer and removes an older memorandum. We do not see its full contents. One page bears a contractor's seal and a handwritten note from an earlier meeting.

He hears a soft knock. Elara stands in the doorway.

**ELARA** You saw the eastern report.

**EDRIC** I saw it.

**ELARA** Then you know it is not complete.

**EDRIC** I know the programme is more complicated than Rex understands.

**ELARA** He is asking to see the records.

**EDRIC** He is asking in a way that will make the council choose sides.

**ELARA** Perhaps the council should choose the truth.

Edric puts the memorandum away.

**EDRIC** You think truth arrives without consequences.

**ELARA** No. I think silence has consequences too.

They hold each other's gaze. Neither wins.

## SCENE 9 — EXT. AVELOR PALACE — NIGHT

A wide view of the palace illuminated against the dark. Beyond the walls, the city continues: late buses, a closing market, a distant siren, music from a courtyard.

Inside a lit window, Rex carries sleeping puppy Milo toward his room. In another, Amara copies dates into a notebook. In the king's study, the drawer is locked again.

The kingdom looks peaceful from a distance.

**REX (V.O.)** A crown can make a man believe he sees the whole kingdom. Sometimes it only means he has learned to look from very far away.

Cut to black.

**END — EPISODE 001.**
