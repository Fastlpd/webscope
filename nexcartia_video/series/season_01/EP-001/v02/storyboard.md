# EP-001 storyboard — The House of Kings (v02)

## Global visual bible
Cinematic live-action realism; 16:9 master (safe central composition for 9:16 crops); 24 fps; natural skin texture; consistent faces; restrained film grain; slow dolly, measured tracking, selective handheld only in the city. Palette: midnight navy, warm ivory limestone, antique gold, carved walnut, bronze and culturally grounded woven textiles. Avoid fantasy armour, glittering excess, generic European-only castle design, plastic skin, face drift, extra fingers, distorted architecture, random crowds, visible AI text, logos and watermarks.

**Identity lock:** No approved character reference portraits exist yet. Use the same provisional appearance description in every shot and do not claim generated faces are canon until the creator approves them.

## Shot list and prompts
1. **Dawn establishing:** Sweeping aerial-to-ground glide toward Avelor Palace at sunrise; pale limestone, restrained gold, navy banners, bronze details, African-inspired carved wood and woven motifs; capital city waking beyond walls; majestic, grounded, elegant.
2. **Milo and Rex:** Intimate medium close-up in sunlit conservatory; 22-year-old Rex with light warm complexion, thick dark curls, dark eyes, lean athletic build, ivory shirt; very young warm-brown puppy with small white chest mark; puppy tugs report; warm sibling-comedy energy.
3. **Amara enters:** Princess Amara, 19–20, light warm complexion, dark expressive eyes, related facial structure to Rex but distinct styling, muted teal day dress; hands him tea and points to report; natural intimate blocking.
4. **Family breakfast:** King Edric, Queen Elara, Rex, Amara and Adrian around a long but lived-in dining table; royal elegance without stiff ceremony; subtle tension rises as flood report is discussed.
5. **Eastern district:** Respectful street-level tracking through a lively working neighbourhood; repaired and unrepaired buildings, shopkeepers, schoolchildren, tailor, fruit seller; Rex and Amara listen to residents without a parade or saviour framing.
6. **Resident testimony:** Close two-shot of a mother showing a damp ceiling patch and a creased repair reference paper; Rex listens carefully, Amara records the reference; no exaggerated suffering.
7. **Archive discovery:** Amara and archivist Mara Venn compare ledger dates in a warm timber archive; repeated dates visible only as indistinct marks, add legible text in post if required.
8. **Soren's briefing:** Elegant but shadowed king's study; Soren places a folder before Edric; Adrian watches, uneasy; polished political tension, no villain caricature.
9. **Rex and Amara corridor:** Puppy Milo toddles between siblings; they share a quiet exchange about honesty and family; gentle tracking shot.
10. **Queen and King:** Elara challenges Edric privately; he puts a sealed memorandum back in a drawer; restrained close-ups and negative space.
11. **Final kingdom tableau:** Night palace windows and active city beyond; layered depth, warm palace light against cool city dusk; separate windows reveal Rex with sleeping puppy, Amara with notebook, Edric alone.
12. **End frame:** Black title card added in post; no generated text.

## Audio and edit
Use original orchestral score: restrained strings, low piano, subtle hand percussion and warm woodwind textures. Do not imitate a named composer or existing soundtrack. Keep dialogue intelligible. City ambience should feel specific and human: buses, distant vendors, workshop tools, evening courtyard music. No generic crowd roar. Add readable documents, signage and subtitles in post-production.
