# EP-001 storyboard and generation prompts

**Global style:** photorealistic cinematic drama, grounded royal architecture, restrained emotion, tactile detail. 2.39:1 widescreen. Palace palette: midnight navy, pearl stone, restrained warm gold. Street palette: cool blue-grey, muted amber. No fantasy effects, embedded text, fake logos, or sensational poverty imagery.

## Character locks
Rex: 22-year-old man, lean athletic build, warm medium-brown skin, thick dark curly hair, dark expressive eyes; dark navy formal jacket, ivory shirt, charcoal trousers, black shoes. Keep face, hairline, build and wardrobe continuous.
Milo: medium-sized warm-brown dog, small white chest mark, dark eyes, simple dark collar and lead. Calm and loyal. Preserve exact design across all shots. Use dog-safe food and water only.
Father: late 50s/early 60s, silver at temples, tailored dark suit. Mother: early/mid-50s, pearl/champagne dress. Older guard: 50s, practical uniform.

## Shot prompts
1. **Palace / 0:00–0:40:** Wide slow push through an empty grand palace corridor at night, rain crawling down tall windows, navy stone and warm practical lamps, distant rehearsal music, photorealistic 2.39:1.
2. **Rex overhears / 0:40–1:20:** Rex, 22, warm medium-brown skin, thick dark curls, navy formal jacket and ivory shirt, stands outside a half-open study door holding a folded drawing; warm light cuts across his stunned face, shallow focus, restrained performance.
3. **Family table / 1:20–3:00:** Rex stands at the end of a long formal dining table; father in dark tailored suit and mother in pearl-champagne dress sit apart; candlelight, symmetrical wide shifting to intimate close-ups, quiet tension.
4. **Milo enters / 3:00–3:35:** Milo, medium-sized warm-brown dog with small white chest mark and dark collar, enters carrying his lead; Rex crouches and gently takes it; intimate warm close-up, natural canine movement.
5. **Palace corridor / 3:35–4:10:** Rex in same outfit walks with Milo beside him, small travel bag, two guards in a long service corridor; slow lateral tracking, pale dawn through high windows.
6. **Envelope / 4:10–4:35:** Close-up of older guard's hand offering sealed cream envelope to Rex; focus shifts to Rex's eyes, exit doors behind him.
7. **Gates / 4:35–5:20:** Iron palace gates open to a misty city street at dawn; Rex, same face and navy outfit, small bag over shoulder, Milo at his side; warm palace behind, cool city ahead.
8. **Gate closes / 5:20–5:55:** Rex stands still as a dark car drives away and gates close; hold on his face, emphasize latch sound, no melodramatic tears.
9. **Milo care / 5:55–6:35:** At a modest roadside shelter Rex checks Milo's paws and offers clean water; warm-brown coat, white chest mark, dark collar; natural morning light, gentle handheld close-up.
10. **Market help / 6:35–7:30:** Rex helps a tired delivery rider gather household items from a fallen crate; Milo waits calmly near wall; ordinary market street, human-scale camera, believable background movement.
11. **Rented room / 7:30–8:15:** Modest clean room, one table and chair, narrow window; Rex lays a folded blanket for Milo; subdued evening light, wide composition.
12. **Unsent message / 8:15–9:20:** Rex sits on floor beside Milo, records a voice message to his mother then stops; phone screen content must be added in post, not generated; soft side light, intimate close-up.
13. **Notebook / 9:20–10:15:** Rex writes in a small notebook under a warm desk lamp; do not generate legible writing. Add exact sentence in post: “What do people need when nobody is coming to help?”
14. **Watcher / 10:15–10:50:** Rain-specked window looking onto a dark vehicle across the street; indistinct adult silhouette watches the room and raises a phone, identity concealed.
15. **Final image / 10:50–11:10:** Rex sits beside Milo, Milo rests his head on Rex's foot; Rex looks toward window; hold, fade room tone, cut to black.

## Sound and edit
Open with rain, distant quartet and clock tick. Cut the quartet when Rex is discovered. Match cut the palace door latch to the gate latch. After exile, replace palace clock with distant traffic and Milo's collar sound. Keep strings/piano restrained; no named-score imitation. End with Milo breathing and the unknown car engine fading. Add dialogue, signs, messages and notebook writing in post-production.
