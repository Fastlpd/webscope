# EP-001 — The Heir Who Was Sent Away
**Format:** cinematic drama | **Runtime target:** 10–12 minutes | **Status:** screenplay draft

**Logline:** On the night his parents decide to send him away, a young royal heir discovers that the palace which raised him can also close its doors on him. With only a small bag and Milo, his loyal dog, Rex must face a world where his title offers no protection.

**Dramatic question:** When every familiar form of protection is taken away, what remains of a person?

**Emotional promise:** An intimate tragedy told through small details: a door that does not open, a familiar corridor suddenly guarded, a mother unable to meet her son's eyes, and Milo waiting beside Rex.

**Boundaries:** The princess does not appear. Nexcartia does not yet exist. The full reason for the exile remains hidden. Rex is 22. Milo is canonical. No product placement.
