# EP-001 continuity sheet

## Canon
- Rex is 22, a young royal heir sent away by his parents.
- His dog is named **Milo**. This name is approved canon for all episodes and shorts.
- The princess does not appear. Nexcartia has not yet been founded.
- The full motive behind the exile and the statement Rex refused to sign remain unknown.

## Visual locks
- Rex: youthful 22-year-old, lean athletic build, warm medium-brown skin, thick dark curls, dark eyes; navy formal jacket, ivory shirt, charcoal trousers, black shoes. Jacket becomes damp and creased over the day.
- Milo: medium-sized warm-brown dog, small white chest marking, dark attentive eyes, simple dark collar and lead. Preserve coat, size, markings and temperament. Confirm exact breed/reference image before video generation.
- Father: late 50s/early 60s, silver at temples, tailored dark suit, controlled posture.
- Mother: early/mid-50s, pearl/champagne formal dress, restrained jewellery, emotionally conflicted.
- Older guard: 50s, practical uniform, humane but cautious.

## Timeline and props
One continuous night into the following evening. Props: folded dog drawing, sealed envelope, travel bag, phone, notebook, water bottle, dog bowl, blanket. Locations: palace east corridor, dining room, service corridor, gates, roadside shelter, market street, rented room.

## End-of-episode knowledge
Rex knows he was sent away but not why. His mother knows more than she says. His father believes exile will teach Rex something, but the audience should suspect another motive. The older guard knows the order was not entirely voluntary. An unknown watcher has located Rex. Milo stays close; do not anthropomorphize him or give him human speech.
