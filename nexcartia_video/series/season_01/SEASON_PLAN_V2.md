## Illustrated episode deliverables
Each episode must contain:
- Complete screenplay, not just a synopsis.
- Scene-by-scene storyboard with camera, framing, action, expression and lighting.
- Separate illustration for each major scene/story beat, using approved character references.
- Illustration prompt file and scene-to-image index.
- Continuity ledger including in-world time, character knowledge, wardrobe, props, relationships and Milo's age.
- Approval checklist. No batch is final until creator review.

A single poster or collage does not count as an illustrated episode. Use a consistent cinematic illustrated-realism style. Do not generate video or publish until the creator approves the script, reference assets and illustrations.
