# CHARACTER VISUAL CANON — NEXCARTIA
Status: Visual direction approved in principle; individual permanent faces await creator selection.

## Family resemblance rule
The creator requires consistent, recognisable faces from scene to scene. The royal family should visibly resemble one another and have fair-to-light warm complexions. Rex is the primary face anchor. Amara, his sister, shares the strongest sibling resemblance; Adrian, his cousin raised as a brother, shares broader extended-family traits. They must look related, not identical. Lighting must not arbitrarily change complexion.

## Identity anchors
- **Rex:** 22 at exile; fair-to-light warm complexion; thick dark curls; dark expressive eyes; tall, lean athletic build; gentle but defined facial structure.
- **Princess Amara:** 19–20; fair-to-light warm complexion; dark expressive eyes; clear sibling resemblance to Rex, with her own distinct styling.
- **Prince Adrian:** 23–24; fair-to-light warm complexion; dark hair; shared extended-family traits, but a distinct face and sharper styling.
- **King Edric:** late 50s; dignified weathered face; fair-to-light warm complexion; dark hair threaded with grey.
- **Queen Elara:** early-to-mid 50s; graceful features, warm eyes, fair-to-light warm complexion, dark hair with subtle age changes.
- **Milo:** very young puppy in palace opening; warm brown coat, small white chest marking, dark expressive eyes and simple collar. Exact breed and canonical face await creator selection.
- **Melody:** fair-to-light complexion direction but a unique face; never reuse Amara's identity.
- **Sophia, Victoria and Jane:** separate individual faces and styling to be designed and approved before major scenes.

## Required permanent reference set per character
1. Canonical portrait, neutral expression, controlled background.
2. Three-quarter full-body reference for proportions and posture.
3. Expression sheet: neutral, joy, concern, anger, grief and surprise.
4. Wardrobe sheet for each story era.
5. Age progression references where chronology requires them.
6. Voice, movement and mannerism note.

## Milo continuity
Palace opening: very young puppy, small body and puppy proportions. Exile: still a puppy. Early rebuilding: age only as elapsed story time supports it. Later adolescent/adult appearance only in a later timeline. Never use adult Milo in palace scenes.

## Scene illustration contract
Each illustration prompt includes approved reference IDs, age in timeline, locked complexion and facial traits, wardrobe, location, time/weather, action, expression, composition, lens/framing, lighting, palette and aspect ratio. Use image-reference conditioning where supported. Text-only generic prompts are insufficient for identity-critical shots. Reject faces that materially differ from approved references. Map each illustration to its exact script scene.

Candidate portraits are not canon until creator approval. Approved identity assets cannot be automatically overwritten.
