# Nexcartia Follow-up Shorts Pack
## Series: Rex — Before the Store Had a Name
Status: Draft for human review. Opening sequence for vertical short-form video; not published.

## Creative objective
Make viewers care about Rex before they know his royal identity or see Nexcartia succeed. The opening shorts follow Rex and his loyal dog through modest street life as Rex builds the first version of Nexcartia with limited money, time, equipment, and connections. The dog is not a prop: it is Rex's closest companion and a steady measure of his kindness. Nexcartia begins through the practical problems Rex notices and the useful things he wants to make easier for people.

## Canon and continuity lock
- **Rex:** young adult man, restrained and observant, tired but not hopeless; short dark hair; plain charcoal knit or worn dark jacket, muted olive trousers, clean-but-worn white sneakers, small weathered backpack. Keep facial features, hairstyle, build, and outfit stable within the opening sequence. Do not dress him like a prince during the street-life timeline.
- **Dog:** medium-sized tan-and-cream mixed-breed dog, darker muzzle, gentle brown eyes, floppy ears, white toes, plain dark harness and simple leash. Loyal, attentive, calm; never anthropomorphized or given human expressions.
- **Location:** modest urban neighbourhood, small rented room / sheltered entryway, worn wood table, basic bowl, small food container, used phone or low-cost laptop, notebook with rough sketches. The setting should feel lived-in and respectful, not a theatrical slum set.
- **Palette:** cool slate-blue evening and soft window light; warm amber light around the meal; one restrained teal accent appears later on Rex's notebook or the first Nexcartia screen.
- **Camera language:** close human-scale shots, slow controlled push-ins, quiet handheld texture, shallow depth of field only when motivated. No drone shots, luxury cars, glossy palace imagery, or rapid montage in these shorts.
- **Audio:** rain / distant traffic / room tone, leash ring, bowl placed gently on the floor, pencil on paper, laptop fan. Use restrained original or licensed ambient music only.
- **Safety:** Rex's meal can be plain rice, bread, or another modest human meal; the dog receives dog-safe food and clean water in its own bowl. Do not show Rex feeding the dog unsafe leftovers, chocolate, onions, grapes, cooked bones, or seasoned food. Do not portray starvation, animal abuse, or humiliating poverty as spectacle.
- **Brand rule:** no fake product listing, sales dashboard, customer review, sales count, price, or order confirmation. The first brand mark can be a rough handwritten "Nexcartia" note, then a basic draft storefront only after Rex has visibly worked toward it.

## Release order
1. **SHORT-REX-001 — Half a Meal, Two Hearts**: establish Rex's care for the dog and the quiet dignity of their daily life. End with Rex turning a small notebook over and writing down a problem he wants to solve.
2. **SHORT-REX-002 — The Last Coins**: Rex counts his remaining money, chooses essentials, and makes a realistic plan rather than abandoning the dog or pretending everything is easy. The notebook follows him.
3. **SHORT-REX-003 — One Useful Thing**: a small everyday problem sparks the first Nexcartia idea. Rex researches options on a worn phone / borrowed laptop and writes down what a genuinely useful store should do.
4. **SHORT-REX-004 — A Store With No Audience**: Rex creates a rough storefront, checks product descriptions, fixes a mistake, and keeps going despite no immediate attention. Do not imply real catalogue items until verified.
5. **SHORT-REX-005 — The First Honest Reply**: an early fictional inquiry tests Rex's patience and honesty. He admits what he still needs to verify rather than making a false promise.
6. **SHORT-REX-006 — The Quiet First Order**: only use an order event if the episode is clearly labelled fiction and does not imply a real Nexcartia sales milestone. The emotional beat is reliability, not sudden success.
7. **SHORT-REX-007 — Someone Notices**: a charming unknown visitor / customer asks a thoughtful question that hints the store may reach beyond Rex's small neighbourhood.
8. **SHORT-REX-008 — The Girl Beyond the Gates**: introduce the princess only after viewers understand Rex's values. Their first connection is ordinary, kind, and unforced.

## Edit and publishing notes
- Total target duration: 42 seconds. Adjust only after the generated clips are reviewed.
- Generate each shot independently using the full prompt and continuity lock; do not assume the video model remembers earlier generations.
- Keep dialogue sparse. Use natural room sound under music. Do not use trending dance formats, meme sounds, nostalgia audio, or unlicensed music by default.
- Use readable subtitles in safe areas, high contrast, and avoid covering the dog, notebook, or hands.
- Do not let AI generate readable brand text. Add "Nexcartia" and on-screen captions in the editor.
- Suggested title: "He Had Almost Nothing Left. His Dog Still Came First."
- Caption: "Before Nexcartia had a name, Rex had one promise to keep: care for the companion who stayed. This is where his story begins."
- CTA: "Follow the beginning. Part 2: The Last Coins."
- Label as a fictional cinematic brand story where needed; do not present it as a real founder testimonial or real customer case.
